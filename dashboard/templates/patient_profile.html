ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

Deactivate account

Deactivating your account signs you out and prevents future sign-ins. Your appointment and refund history is retained for record-keeping; this is not a permanent deletion of database records.

  • You cannot deactivate while you have an appointment that has not yet ended.
  • You can review your appointment history before proceeding.
  • Your current login session is revoked when deactivation succeeds.
